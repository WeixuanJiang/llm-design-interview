## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The shell is recognizably AI-generated: bordered hero card, gradient/orbit illustration, floating translucent nodes, four stat cards, three identical mode cards, repeated uppercase eyebrows, and broad card grids. The mission workspace is much stronger and product-specific because architecture nodes, decision records, stress events, and contextual evidence embody the actual learning loop.

**Deterministic scan:** 115 findings: 5 warnings and 110 advisories. Warnings were one overused-font finding, one single-font finding, two layout-property transitions (`width` at line 199 and `padding-right` at line 318), and one colored-glow finding. Advisories were 28 radius, 36 font-size, and 45 color mismatches against DESIGN.md, plus one numbered-marker heuristic. The 109 token-conformance advisories are real evidence that the prototype vocabulary is broader than the documented system, but repeated values inflate the count. The numbered markers are a likely false positive because they represent an actual learning sequence. Inter/single-font warnings are stylistic, not functional defects for this product register.

**Visual overlays:** No reliable overlay is available. Fresh browser inspection failed (`CDP response channel closed`; headless Chrome exit 21), and the separate browser-evidence attempt hung and was interrupted. Source inspection and deterministic scanning were used as the fallback.

## Overall Impression

The core concept is unusually strong, but the prototype spends too much visual and interaction budget on a generic dashboard shell while the distinctive build-break-revise loop remains mostly simulated. The biggest opportunity is to make the mission workspace real, task-dominant, and accessible.

## What's Working

1. Mode adaptation changes pressure, language, feedback, and evidence coherently without fragmenting the product.
2. Architecture nodes, decisions, metrics, and the stress event make the learning mechanic specific and credible.
3. Semantic color roles, stable structure, and reduced-motion handling provide a disciplined foundation.

## Priority Issues

### P1: The build-and-revise loop is a false affordance

Component clicks announce additions without changing the graph; zoom, node menus, and reasoning edit controls are inert; stages change styling without distinct content. Implement a real graph model, add/remove/connect, decision editing, undo/redo, autosave, stage-specific tasks, feedback, revision, rerun, and comparison. Suggested command: `$impeccable harden preview.html`.

### P1: The mission workspace overloads the learner

The open palette, eight components, toolbar, stepper, six-node graph, decision record, metrics, warnings, and open coach compete at the highest-reasoning moment. Collapse the coach by default, reveal the palette through an Add action, gate future stages, and move node details into a contextual inspector. Suggested command: `$impeccable distill preview.html`.

### P1: Accessibility misses the WCAG 2.2 AA commitment

Clickable graph elements are not keyboard focusable, focus treatment is incomplete, toast and stress updates are not announced, icon controls lack names, the graph has no accessible outline, and mobile labels/nodes are too small. Add semantic controls, a keyboard-complete outline view, visible focus, `aria-live`, 44px targets, and readable mobile sizing. Suggested command: `$impeccable audit preview.html`.

### P2: First-run direction and mode choice are duplicated

Top-bar mode controls, three mode cards, Start session, Start a mission, Explore learning path, and resume compete. Ask intent once, then make home a mode-specific next-action surface with one dominant recommendation. Suggested command: `$impeccable onboard preview.html`.

### P2: The generic dashboard shell dilutes the product

Decorative gradients, orbit art, repeated cards, uppercase eyebrows, and broad bordered/shadowed surfaces conflict with the Interactive Engineering Workbench direction. Replace hero decoration with a real mission artifact, remove low-value stats, reduce repeated cards, and normalize the token drift flagged by the detector. Suggested command: `$impeccable polish preview.html`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan, beginner AI engineer:** Competing start actions obscure the recommended path; AWS names, P95, QPS, HNSW, and nDCG appear before enough inline definition; pre-populated progress hides the genuine first-run state.

**Alex, interview candidate:** Only Ctrl+Enter accelerates work. There is no keyboard diagram workflow, duplicate, undo, command palette, or efficient original-versus-revised comparison during a timed session.

**Riley, production engineer:** Inert add/zoom/menu controls break trust immediately. Refresh recovery, failed saves, malformed graphs, real evidence exploration, mitigation, and post-change verification are absent.

## Minor Observations

- Hero tracking at `-0.052em` exceeds the documented `-0.04em` floor.
- Broad shadows appear on already bordered surfaces.
- Indigo-violet gradients weaken the one-action-color rule.
- Mode cards combine article click behavior with nested buttons.
- Empty, loading, offline, save-failure, and no-progress states are absent.
- Absolute graph positioning will not handle long labels, localization, or arbitrary topology well.

## Questions to Consider

1. What if Home were the next engineering decision rather than a dashboard linking to the real experience?
2. Should the first implementation prioritize a genuine end-to-end mission loop over breadth across five top-level sections?
3. Should the visual cleanup preserve the current shell or let the mission workspace become the dominant product identity?
