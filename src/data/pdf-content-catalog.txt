## 1. Rights and content-use note

The PDF states that it is copyrighted and that reproduction or distribution requires permission, except for limited uses permitted by law. Before publishing lessons, code, figures, tables, sample answers, or company case-study material, confirm that the project has the necessary licence or permission.

Safe implementation approach:

- Use this catalog as a **topic map and source reference**.
- Write original explanations, exercises, diagrams, questions, rubrics, and scenarios.
- Do not copy long passages, sample answers, diagrams, code listings, or tables into the website without permission.
- Attribute licensed source material and maintain a source-page field in the CMS.

## 2. Recommended website content model

| Website area | What belongs here from the PDF | Active-learning treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | Study plan, track summaries, featured chapters and missions | Diagnostic entry point, recommended next activity, resume progress |
| **Learn** | Chapter overviews and `Core Concepts` sections | Predict-first questions, short explanations, parameter labs, concept checks |
| **Missions** | End-to-end design questions, failure taxonomy, applied case studies | Requirements -> estimation -> architecture -> stress event -> revision |
| **Practice** | `Tough Questions`, Top System Design Questions, mock interview and question index | Timed answers, AI follow-ups, whiteboard drills, estimation drills |
| **Progress** | Study-plan milestones, difficulty tags, interview scoring dimensions and competency categories | Mastery graph, weak-skill recommendations, spaced repetition, revision history |

### Content package generated from one source topic

Each important topic should be transformed into a reusable package:

1. **Concept lesson** - a short original explanation with one visual model.
2. **Prediction prompt** - ask the learner to choose or explain before revealing content.
3. **Micro-practice** - calculation, configuration, ordering, matching, or debugging task.
4. **Interview drill** - an open question plus adaptive follow-ups.
5. **Production stress event** - a latency, cost, quality, security, or reliability failure.
6. **Revision task** - learner improves the answer or architecture after feedback.
7. **Mastery signal** - store the competency and evidence used to update Progress.

## 3. Mode transformation rules

| Mode | How the same PDF material should be presented |
|---|---|
| **Beginner** | Guided steps, constrained choices, glossary, immediate feedback, worked calculations and partial architecture templates |
| **Interview** | Blank or lightly scaffolded workspace, timer, requirement clarification, AI interviewer challenges, communication rubric and concise model after submission |
| **Production** | Existing architecture, traces/logs/metrics, recent changes, incidents, incomplete evidence, cost/SLA constraints and remediation plan |

A learner can be Beginner in one competency and Production-level in another. Mode should control assistance and scenario presentation, not permanently label the user.

## 16. Content quality checklist

Before publishing a PDF-derived activity:

- The explanation is original and shorter than a passive chapter.
- The learner must act before seeing the solution.
- The activity maps to at least one competency.
- Beginner, Interview and Production behavior is defined where applicable.
- Numeric claims and service-specific facts are independently verified and dated.
- Multiple valid solutions are recognised.
- The rubric evaluates reasoning and trade-offs, not keyword matching.
- Failure and rollback behavior are included for production topics.
- Source section and page metadata are stored.
- Copyright/licensing review is complete.
